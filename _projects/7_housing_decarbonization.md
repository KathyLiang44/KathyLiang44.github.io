## The question

California funds many programs that help households replace gas appliances with electric ones, but participation skews toward affluent homeowners. CARB asked why low-income households and renters are left out, and what the state could change so these programs reach them.

## What I did

1. **Stakeholder interviews.** I led interviews with utilities, contractors, funding agencies, and program implementers about where households drop out of electrification programs.
2. **Analysis.** I coded the interviews to identify systemic gaps, and contributed to reports on aligning program funding cycles and equity requirements.

## What the study found

The final report, published in 2026, found that low-income households face barriers at every stage, and that fixing one stage alone is not enough.

- **Usability decides the payoff.** Energy security improved substantially only for households that could run their new systems and were satisfied with the installation, which is why the report pairs funding with navigators and contractor training.
